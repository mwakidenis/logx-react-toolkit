# Governance

## Maintainer

Mwaki Denis (https://github.com/mwakidenis) is the sole maintainer of @mwakidenis/logx-react.

## Decision-making

The maintainer makes final decisions on design, API, and releases. Community
contributions are welcome and reviewed on their merits.

## Versioning

The project follows semantic versioning. Breaking changes are communicated in
CHANGELOG.md and release notes.

## Sustainability

The project is maintained as open source in the maintainer's spare time.
Sponsorship and contributions help sustain development.
